## About

I'm Fan.Bin (范彬), an architect at **HSBC**. My focus has long been on three things: **big data** — ingestion, modelling, and governance at scale; **high performance** — treating latency and throughput as design constraints rather than something to optimise after launch; and **AI** — getting LLMs into real business flows instead of leaving them in demos.

Outside of work I run on two tracks: **putting AI into real scenarios**, and **turning ideas into things you can click on** — from a 3D shop in the browser to a card game you can install on your phone.

## 🚧 In Progress

### ⚽ AI Football

> Understanding football through AI.

Turning matches and training sessions into computable data: structured events, tactic recognition, and player / lineup performance evaluation.
The goal is to move from *watching* a match to *reading* it — so the conclusions can directly inform training and selection.

`Status: in progress` · write-ups and code coming out gradually

### 📊 AI × Big Data

> Putting an LLM directly on top of the data stack.

Natural-language querying, metric anomaly attribution, and auto-generated analysis — handing the whole **fetch → analyze → report** chain to agents,
so people stop hand-writing SQL and building pivot tables.

`Status: in progress`

| **[kid-shop-3d](https://github.com/BbrotherSEU/kid-shop-3d)** · classroom | Ready in one lesson — no software to install, just open a browser and practise yuan / jiao with the class | `TypeScript` `Three.js` |

**Design principle**: hide the knowledge inside the action. The kid isn't doing a worksheet — they're buying something for real, and if they get the math wrong, they can't pay.
